Principal investigator

Mackenzie Weygandt Mathis, PhD

Associate Professor, EPFL · Bertarelli Foundation Chair of Integrative Neuroscience · ELLIS Scholar

Mackenzie leads the M-Lab of Adaptive Intelligence at EPFL's Campus Biotech in Geneva. She earned her PhD at Harvard University with Naoshige Uchida, was a Rowland Fellow at Harvard from 2017 to 2020, and joined EPFL in 2020. Her group develops open-source tools including DeepLabCut and CEBRA.
