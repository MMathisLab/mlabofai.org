Software

Open-source tools.

We ship our methods as open-source software, so that other labs can measure behavior, model the body and make sense of neural data too.

Pose estimation

DeepLabCut

Markerless pose estimation of user-defined body parts with deep learning, for animals of any species. DeepLabCut supports 3D and multi-animal tracking, and ships the SuperAnimal pretrained foundation models, so you can analyze behavior with few or no labels.

  • Nature Neuroscience 2018
  • Nature Protocols 2019
  • Nature Methods 2022
  • Nature Communications 2024

Representation learning

CEBRA

Self-supervised learning that produces consistent, identifiable latent embeddings of behavioral and neural data, across sessions, animals and recording modalities, with attribution maps to see what drives them.

  • Nature 2023
  • AISTATS 2025

Biomechanics

MusBioMaus

A neuro-musculoskeletal model of the mouse that merges neural control and musculoskeletal modeling, to study adaptive motor control from cortex to individual muscles.
