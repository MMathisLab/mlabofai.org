Open positions.

We welcome people from machine learning, computer vision, engineering and neuroscience. Here is how to get involved.

  • PhD student

    Starting spring 2027

    EPFL PhD students apply through the EDNE, EDEE, EDRS or EDIC doctoral programs and list Prof. Mathis as a potential advisor. Please note that the PI does not respond to pre-screening emails before you apply.

  • Postdoctoral fellows

    Positions are posted here when open. We also welcome candidates with external funding, such as Horizon Europe Swiss or EPFLeaders4impact fellowships.

  • Engineers & research assistants

    Neuro-engineers, software engineers and research assistants work on software, database infrastructure, hardware and experiments, including mouse training. Specific openings are posted here.

  • ELLIS & visiting PhD students

    Students at other universities can seek co-supervision or short research stays. ELLIS applicants should name Prof. Mathis as a potential advisor.

  • Master's projects & theses

    Spring & fall 2027

    Semester projects focus on self-supervised learning for behavioral, neural and cell analysis. Master's theses are open to EPFL students. Email the PI with your CV, transcript and a short statement of interests.

  • Summer internships

    Apply through the Summer@EPFL program.

  • Bachelor's projects

    Typically computational neuroscience or computer vision, with some experimental work. For internships, bachelor's projects and non-EPFL master's requests, contact PhD students in the lab directly; spots fill 6 to 12 months in advance.

Postdoc applications

What to send.

  • A cover letter (one page maximum)
  • A CV with links to your Google Scholar, GitHub or website
  • One publication or preprint
  • A short research direction (about half a page)
  • Names of 2–3 references
