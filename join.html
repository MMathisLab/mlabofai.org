All ways to join

  • PhD student

    EPFL PhD students apply through the EDNE, EDEE, EDRS or EDIC doctoral programs and list Prof. Weygandt Mathis as a potential advisor. Please note that the PI does not respond to pre-screening emails before you apply.

  • Postdoctoral fellows

    Open positions are listed under Current openings. We also welcome candidates with external funding, such as Horizon Europe Swiss or EPFLeaders4impact fellowships.

  • Engineers & research assistants

    Neuro-engineers, software engineers and research assistants work on software, database infrastructure, hardware and experiments, including mouse training. Open positions are listed under Current openings.

  • ELLIS & visiting PhD students

    Students at other universities can seek co-supervision or short research stays. ELLIS applicants should name Prof. Weygandt Mathis as a potential advisor.

  • Master's projects & theses

    Semester projects focus on self-supervised learning for behavioral and neural analysis, and on biomechanics. Master's theses are open to EPFL students. Write directly to the postdocs and PhD students whose work interests you, with your CV, transcript and a short statement of interests.

  • Summer internships

    Apply through the Summer@EPFL program.

  • Bachelor's projects

    Typically computational neuroscience or computer vision, with some experimental work. For internships, bachelor's projects and non-EPFL master's requests, contact PhD students in the lab directly; spots fill 6 to 12 months in advance.

Postdoc applications

What to send.

  • A cover letter (one page maximum)
  • A CV with links to your Google Scholar, GitHub or website
  • One publication or preprint
  • A short research direction (about half a page)
  • Names of 2–3 references
